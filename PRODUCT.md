# Product

## Register

product

## Users
HYROX athletes mid-plan, using the app gym-side. Phone in hand between sets, sweaty and distracted, glancing at today's session and moving on. Taps are fast and one-handed. Planning and coach chat happen too, but the gym glance is the defining context.

## Product Purpose
An AI-generated HYROX training plan in the athlete's pocket. It builds a plan from onboarding, shows what to do today, tracks sessions, and counts down to race day. Success: the athlete knows today's session in under two seconds and trusts the plan.

## Brand Personality
Sharp, fast, athletic. Momentum without noise. Motion is short and decisive (roughly 150 to 250ms, exponential ease-out), confirms what happened, and never makes a gym-side tap feel laggy. References in spirit: Whoop, Strava, Nike Run Club.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS or AI slop: purple gradients, sparkle icons, chatbot shimmer everywhere.
- Gamified clutter: confetti, XP bars, popping badges.
- Bouncy or playful easing, and slow cinematic fades.

## Design Principles
1. Glanceable first. The next thing to do is readable before any motion finishes.
2. Motion explains state. Transitions show where you came from and where you went; loaders show real progress, not decoration.
3. Fast is a feature. Nothing blocks input; animations never delay a tap.
4. AI work is visible but restrained. Generation progress reads as stages of real work, not magic.
5. Earn every effect. If it doesn't clarify or confirm, cut it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast on the dark theme. Every animation collapses to a plain fade or instant change under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Touch targets sized for one-handed, gym-side use.
