# HYROX 12-Week Training Plan — Pro Level

Source: HYROX Academy — "Exemplary 12 Week Training Plan — Pro-Level" PDF (`HYROX-content-file-HYROX_12_Week_Training_Plan_PRO-LEVEL.1717500831.pdf`)

## Terminology & Methodology

### 01 | Planning of Training

**Training Blocks:**
- Week 01-04: Base
- Week 05-08: Pace
- Week 09-10: Accelerate
- Week 11: Prime
- Week 12: Race

### 02 | Programs

**Target Groups:**

- **Beginner**
  - No HYROX Experience
  - Minimal or zero weightlifting experience
  - 10k Run Time > 1 hour (Women & Men)

- **Open**
  - No HYROX Experience needed
  - Understands basic strength/weightlifting movements
  - 10k Time < 1 hour (Women & Men)

- **Pro**
  - Current Experienced Athlete/HYROX Experience
  - Currently on a strength specific program
  - 10k Time < 45 Mins Women + < 40 Mins Men

### 03 | Methodology

**HYROX Foundation**
- 20-40 minutes
- Basic Daily Workout
- Manageable sets and volume

**HYROX Complete**
- 40-60 mins+
- High volume
- Big Sets
- Designed to replicate HYROX Race intensity
- Mental and Physical race prep

**HYROX Engine**
- Includes work:rest ratio
- Focus on pace and/or speed
- Repeating sets designed to develop pacing abilities
- Range between high and low intensity in each workout

**HYROX Power**
- For quality, not for time
- Focus on lower rep ranges with rest as needed
- Basic weightlifting movements and accessory work

**HYROX Aerobic**
- Conversational Effort
- 40-60 Minutes in Duration
- Minimal change in effort throughout

### 04 | Equipment

**Minimum Requirements**
- Sled + Rope
- Kettlebells
- Dumbbells
- Ski Erg and/or Row Erg
- Wall Ball

**Optional Additional**
- Sandbags
- Air Bike or Bike Erg
- Barbell/Squat Rack
- Pull Up Bar
- TRX or Low Rings
- 24/20" Box

## Week 1 — Base

### Monday — Hyrox Foundation
**Workout:**
- 4 RFT:
- 500m Row
- 15 KB Deadlifts (70/53lbs or 32/24kg x 2)
- 20 Wall Balls (20/14lbs or 9/6kg)
- 100m Farmer Carry (70/53lbs or 32/24kg x 2)

**Notes:**
- Pay attention to pace on row, stay consistent
- KB Deadlifts should be heavy but done in 1 set
- Wall Balls should be unbroken
- Go heavier on Farmers Carry if desired

### Tuesday — Hyrox Aerobic
**Workout:**
- 45-60 Minutes Run
- Finish with 6x 20 Second Strides

**Notes:**
- Conversational pace
- During strides, start slow and build to sprint effort for the last 6-10 steps

### Wednesday — Hyrox Foundation
**Workout:**
- 4 RFT:
- 50ft Sled Push - AHAP
- 400m Run
- 15 Headcutters (70/53lbs or 32/24kg)

**Notes:**
- AHAP = As Heavy As Possible
- Headcutters should be heavy enough where you may need to break into 2 sets

### Thursday — Hyrox Power
**Workout:**
- 4x
  - Goblet Squat x 20
  - DB Bench Press x 15
  - Chin Ups x Failure
  - Max Bike Sprint x 30 sec
  - 90 sec Rest
- 4x
  - Bulgarian Split Squats x 10 Each Leg
  - DB Shoulder Press x 15
  - [illegible — redacted with solid red bar in source PDF]
  - DB Curls x 12 Each
  - 200m Loaded Carry (100m Front Rack/100m Farmers Carry)

**Notes:**
- This is for quality, not for time
- Choose weights where the last 3 reps are very challenging
- If unable to do chin ups, do TRX or Ring Rows
- Movements are meant to be done one after the other with minimal rest. Rest 90 seconds after each round
- On the loaded carry, use the same weight for both. Hold KBs or DBs in front rack position for the first 100m, then switch to a farmer carry hold for the second 100m
- Rest as needed throughout

### Friday — Hyrox Foundation
**Workout:**
- For Time: 3 Rounds
- 800m-400m-200m Run
- 30-20-10
- Alt DB Snatch
- [illegible — redacted with solid red bar in source PDF]
- Goblet Lunge
- 50ft/12.5m Sled Push after each round

**Notes:**
- Workout is 3 rounds total, with descending reps each round (Sled Push remains the same throughout)
- Round 1: 800m Run and 30 reps of each Movement
- Round 2: 400m Run and 20 reps of each Movement
- Round 3: 200m Run and 10 reps of each Movement

## Week 2 — Base

### Monday — Hyrox Foundation
**Workout:**
- 25 Min AMRAP:
- 5-10-15-20-25-etc
- KB Deadlift (70/53lbs or 32/24kg x 2)
- KB/DB Front Rack Lunge (70/53lbs or 32/24kg x 2)
- 10-20-30-40-50-etc
- Wall Balls (20/14lbs or 9/6kg)
- *200m Run after each Round

**Notes:**
- Continue up the ladder until you reach 25 minutes
- 200m Run does not change throughout
- Round 1: 5 KB DL/5 FR Lunge/10 Wallballs/200m Run
- Round 2: 10 KB DL/10 FR Lunge/20 Wallballs/200m Run
- Round 3: 15 KB DL/15 FR Lunge/30 Wallballs/200m Run — etc

### Tuesday — Hyrox Aerobic
**Workout:**
- 50-60 Minutes:
- 5:00 Minute Bike
- 5:00 Minute Row
- 5:00 Minute Ski

**Notes:**
- Work for 50-60 minutes total
- Pay attention to pace throughout and stay consistent
- Conversational pace, but you should break a sweat

### Wednesday — Hyrox Foundation
**Workout:**
- 4 RFT:
- 400m Run
- 10 DB Thruster (50/35lbs or 22.5/17.5 kg x 2)
- 12 Burpee Broad Jump

**Notes:**
- DB Thrusters should be unbroken, but they should be heavy enough where you couldn't do 5 more
- Plant hands close to feet in order to maintain the Burpee Broad Jump standard

### Thursday — Hyrox Power
**Workout:**
- For 16 Minutes:
- 100 Cal Row
- Every 2:00 Minutes 10 x Bench Press
- 4x
  - 25 Back Squats (Unbroken)
  - 10 Cal Bike Sprint
  - 2:00 Minutes Rest after each round
  - 400m Suitcase Carry (Switch arms as needed)

**Notes:**
- 100 Cal Row should be a moderate pace
- Stop every 2 Minutes and Foundation 10 x Bench Press. (Build each round)
- Back squats should be unbroken, build each round
- Go immediately into the 10 Cal Bike Sprint
- Suitcase Carry: Switch arms as needed but don't come to a Foundation stop at any point

### Friday — Hyrox Foundation
**Workout:**
- For Time:
- 30-20-10
- Alt DB Snatch
- 50ft/12.5m Sled Pull (Arms Only)
- *200m Farmers Carry after each round (70/53lbs or 32/24kg x 2)

**Notes:**
- 3 Rounds total
- DB Snatch reps decrease each round
- Sled Pull should be AHAP, no walking back
- Farmer Carry should be heavy enough where you need to break at least 1 time during each 200m

## Week 3 — Base

### Monday — Hyrox Foundation
**Workout:**

- 21-18-15-12-9

- *400m Run after each round
- *Every time you break on Wall Balls, Foundation 10 Burpees

**Notes:**
- 5 Rounds, reps decrease each round

- A break on Wall Balls is any time movement stops or ball drops
- Round 1: 50 Wall Balls/21 KBS/400m Run
- Round 2: 40 Wall Balls/18 KBS/400m Run
- Round 3: 30 Wall Balls/15 KBS/400m Run — etc
- (+ penalty burpees if you break on wall balls)

### Tuesday — Hyrox Aerobic
**Workout:**
- 50 Minutes Progressive Run
- Increase pace every 10 Minutes so last 10 Minutes is fastest/hardest effort

**Notes:**
- If you have a GPS watch, use it and take splits to record data
- The last 10 mins should feel harder than race effort

### Wednesday — Hyrox Foundation
**Workout:**
- For Time:
- 100m Farmer Carry
- 21-18-15-12-9-6-3
- DB Hang Clean*
- DB Push Press*
- DB Front Squat*
- *(50/35lbs or 22.5/17.5 kg x 2)

**Notes:**
- 7 Rounds total
- Each round starts with a 100m Farmer Carry
- This will be grippy!
- Use the same weight throughout for all movements

### Thursday — Hyrox Power
**Workout:**
- 4x
  - Barbell RDL x 8
  - DB Split Squat Jump x 10
  - 100ft/25m Sled Sprint
  - 1:30 Minutes Rest
- 4x
  - Bench Press x 20
  - KBS x 20
  - 10 Cal Ski Sprint
  - 1:30 Minutes Rest

**Notes:**
- For quality, not for time
- Build RDL weight
- DBs should be light for Split Squat Jump
- Sled Push: Use 50% of the weight you normally use in training
- Rest after each Foundation round
- Bench Press: Build each round
- Movements should be back to back with rest after each round

### Friday — Hyrox Foundation
**Workout:**
- 4 RFT:
- 20 DB Front Rack Lunge (50/35lbs or 22.5/17.5 kg x 2)
- 15 HR Push Ups
- 10 Cal Ski
- 3 Wall Walks

**Notes:**
- Lunges should be unbroken
- Push Ups should be STRICT: No sagging core, hips and shoulders in line
- HR = Hand Release at bottom of push up
- **Wall Walks:**
  - Start on ground with feet against wall.
  - Push up and walk hands towards the wall and feet up the wall.
  - Don't arch back, keep neutral pelvis
  - Get nose as close to the wall as possible while maintaining technique

**Workout:**
- 4 RFT:
- 400m Ski/Row
- 400m Run
- 100ft/25m Sled Push AHAP

**Notes:**
- Ski/Row: Moderate Pace

**Workout:**
- 4x
  - Split Squat x 10 each
  - KB Deadlift x 20
  - DB Bench Press x 20
  - 1:00 Minute Rest
- 4x
  - 10 Cal Ski
  - 5-8 Strict Pull Ups

  - 1:00 Minute Rest

**Notes:**

- Each round should end with a 10 Cal Sprint

- Round 2: 6 KBS/6 Headcutter/6 Burpee Broad Jump/10 Cal Sprint

- 400m Run

**Notes:**
- Each round should be a build to a 90% effort

### Wednesday — Hyrox Aerobic
**Workout:**
- E2MOM:
- 5000 Meters Row
- 5 x Burpee Over Rower

**Notes:**
- Starting at 0:00, Foundation 5 Burpees over the rower

### Thursday — Hyrox Engine
**Workout:**
- 6 x 400m Run
- 2:00 Minutes Rest after each Run

**Notes:**
- Each 400m should be faster than race pace
- Record each 400m split

**Workout:**
- For Time:

  - 150ft DB Lunge (50/35lbs or 22.5/17.5 kg x 2)

- The 1000m Row or Ski is to be done once as a buy in
- The 100/75 Cal Bike is to be done once as a cash out
- DB Lunges can be done as Front Rack or to your sides
- The Sled Push can be broken up in any way throughout
- Weight on the sled should be AHAP (As Heavy As Possible)

## Week 6 — Pace

### Monday — Hyrox Foundation
**Workout:**
- 3 RFT:
- 200m Farmer Carry (70/53lbs or 32/24kg x 2)
- 15 KBS (70/53lbs or 32/24kg)
- 30 Goblet Squat (70/53lbs or 32/24kg)
- 400 Meters Ski or Row

**Notes:**
- Use the same weight throughout: 1 KB for KBS and Goblet Squat/2 KBs for Farmers Carry
- Farmers Carry weight should be heavy enough where you need to set the KBs down 1-2 times

### Tuesday — Hyrox Engine
**Workout:**
- 20 Minutes Alt EMOM (50 sec on/10 sec off)
- Front Rack KB Lunge (53/35lbs or 24/16kg x 2)
- Strict Pull Ups/Push Ups
- 200 Meters Run
- Rest

**Notes:**
- Alternate movements each minute
- Work 50 seconds, use the 10 seconds rest to transition
- Do a max set of strict pull ups followed by push ups in the time remaining
- If you are unable to do strict pull ups, do push ups only

### Wednesday — Hyrox Aerobic
**Workout:**
- 60 Minutes Run
- Within the 60 Minutes window, Foundation 2x 15 Min Pick Ups

**Notes:**
- The 15 Minute pick ups can be done at any time
- Pace should be lactate threshold hold, or a pace you can maintain for 15 minutes just below the point of discomfort

### Thursday — Hyrox Engine
**Workout:**
- 3 Rounds: 1:00 Minute on/1:00 Minute off
- Sled Push
- Sled Pull
- DB Box Over
- Headcutter (70/53lbs or 32/24kg x 2)
- Push Ups

**Notes:**
- 1 Minute at each movement station
- Alternate movements
- 1 Minute rest between each
- You should be working HARD the entirety of the minute

### Friday — Hyrox Complete
**Workout:**
- For Time:
- 100/75 Cal Bike or Ski
- 100 Back Squat (135/95lbs or 60/45kg)
- 1 Mile Run
- 100 KBS (53/35lbs or 24/16kg)
- 100/75 Cal Bike or Ski
- 100 Headcutters (53/35lbs or 24/16kg)
- *Every break complete 100m Farmer Carry (70/53lbs or 32/24kg x 2)

**Notes:**
- Back Squats: Choose a weight that you can get at least a set of 30 unbroken while fresh
- KBS and Headcutter weight should be slightly lighter than usual
- Every time you break, complete 100m Farmers Carry (Only applies for back squat, KBS, and Headcutters)
- It is considered a break on back squats if you put the bar down
- It is considered a break on KBS and Headcutters if movement stops

## Week 12 — Race

### Monday — Hyrox Foundation
**Workout:**
- 4 RFT:
- 500m Row
- 20 KB Deadlift (70/53lbs or 32/24kg x 2)
- 100m Farmer Carry (70/53lbs or 32/24kg x 2)
- 50ft/12.5m Sled Pull

**Notes:**
- Moderate Pace
- Practice different types of sled pull technique: arms only, full body, walk back
- Go lighter on KB Deadlift and Farmer Carry if you want to lower the intensity

### Tuesday — Hyrox Aerobic
**Workout:**
- 40 Min AMRAP Machine Cals:
- 10-15-20-25-30-etc
- Rotate between Ski/Bike + Row

**Notes:**
- Moderate Pace
- Go by feel, if you feel good and want to add intensity
- Do the first 10 Cals of each movement as a sprint or hard effort followed by recovery

### Wednesday — Hyrox Engine
**Workout:**
- 8x
  - 200m Build to sprint
  - 10 Burpee Broad Jump
  - 1:00 Minute Rest

**Notes:**
- These should feel fresh
- Build to a sprint
- Adjust the number of Burpee Broad Jumps as desired

### Thursday — Hyrox Aerobic
**Workout:**
- 45 Minutes Easy Run

**Notes:**
- This should be a "Feel good" Jog.
- Option to switch to Row, Ski or Bike

### Friday — Hyrox Aerobic
**Workout:**
- Optional:
- 20-30 Minutes Shake Out Run

**Notes:**
- Totally optional depending on how you're feeling
- Only if you think your legs will benefit from a quick/easy run
- Don't stress about getting it in
